About AeroPredict

AeroPredict is a modern, AI-powered system designed to predict flight delays based on historical flight data.

How it works

Our machine learning model analyzes key factors like the scheduled departure time, the airline, the origin, and the destination to estimate the probability of a delay. By utilizing historical flight performance, we can help travelers plan their itineraries safely and effectively.

The Dataset

The model was trained on millions of flight records. All the required preprocessing, one-hot encoding, and hyperparameter tuning were carefully applied using Scikit-Learn to ensure highest possible accuracy.
